Konrad Jakob Mueller

Hi!

I’m Konrad. I’m a PhD student at Imperial College London, supervised by Lukas Gonon, and a part-time quantitative research associate at J.P. Morgan. I am part of the Oxford-Imperial Centre for Doctoral Training in Mathematics of Random Systems.

I am interested in machine learning, quantitative finance, and the intersection of those two fields. My research is focussed on developing efficient and robust techniques for solving stochastic control problems, in particular hedging problems.
